Your wedding paper trail

A simple timeline to follow

  1. 3–6 months beforeGather PSA documents

    CENOMAR and birth certificates for both of you.

  2. 1–2 months beforeApply for the marriage license

    We prepare your checklist and forms ahead of your city hall visit.

  3. 1–2 weeks beforeLock in transport

    Confirm the bridal car, guest vans and pick-up times.

  4. After the weddingUpdate your records

    Request your PSA marriage certificate, then update your name on IDs.

Requirements and processing times vary by city hall and agency. We confirm the exact list for your location when you message us.
